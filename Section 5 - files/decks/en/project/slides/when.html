When do we need the chi-square test?

We often need to know whether two traits (qualitative variables) of a population are related.

Questions like these

?

Is income level related to education level?

?

Is eye colour related to hair colour in a population?

?

Is academic achievement related to family income?

When to use chi-square

To test whether two qualitative variables are related: two nominal variables, or one nominal and one ordinal.

Nominal × Nominal

Nominal × Ordinal
