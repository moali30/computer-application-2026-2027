The procedure

Hypotheses and the decision rule

H0

The null hypothesis

No significant relationship between the two variables (they are independent).

H1

The alternative hypothesis

A significant relationship between the two variables (they are not independent).

1

State the hypotheses

2

Run the test in SPSS

3

Compare Sig with the significance level α

Sig ≥ α

Accept H0, reject H1

No significant relationship: the variables are independent.

Sig < α

Reject H0, accept H1

The variables are significantly related.
