The measure

Cronbach's alpha

Reliability means the items are consistent: a student who agrees with one item tends to agree with the similar ones.

Cronbach's Alpha

Measures the internal consistency of Likert items. It runs from 0 to 1; the closer to 1, the more reliable.

AlphaVerdict
0.90 +Excellent
0.80 – 0.89Good
0.70 – 0.79Acceptable
0.60 – 0.69Weak
below 0.60Unacceptable

The usual minimum in research: 0.70
