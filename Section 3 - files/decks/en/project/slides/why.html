The idea

A measuring tool must be consistent

Everyday example

An unreliable scale

You step on a scale three times in one minute and it reads:

70 kg

75 kg

68 kg

Your weight did not change, so the scale cannot be trusted.

In our case

An unreliable questionnaire

If a student who likes e-learning agrees with some items and rejects similar ones, the items are not measuring the same thing.

That is what a reliability check catches before the survey goes to 400 students.
