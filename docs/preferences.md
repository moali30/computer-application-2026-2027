# Mohamed's preferences for the sections

Collected from the original claude.ai project and from the Section 3 conversation (October 2026).

- He teaches the Computer Application (SPSS) lab sections at Horus University, 2026/2027.
- Every section becomes professional slides: **one Arabic version (RTL) and the same deck in English**.
- All sections share one design system so they look alike. The design basis is **Horus University's colours** (the current palette is a draft until the logo and official colours are supplied).
- Cover and footer show **course + section only**. No instructor name.
- Content comes from the section document he supplies. Teaching extras go in speaker notes.
- He talks in Egyptian Arabic; reply to him in Egyptian Arabic.
- He likes being asked short clarifying questions, each with options and a recommended one, and then he approves (for Section 3 he answered "موافق علي كل ما تم التوصية به").
- Added questions should be **applied**: each one should come with a real-life scenario that explains why we run this analysis, without making the section too long.
- Deliverables per section: both decks, plus the Arabic deck exported as `.pptx` and `.ppsx` in this repo.
