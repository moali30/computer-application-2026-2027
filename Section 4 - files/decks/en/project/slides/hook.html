Before we start

3.7 out of 5: do students really approve?

We asked 12 students about e-learning and their average attitude was 3.7, above the neutral 3. Ask 12 other students and we might get 2.9.

Before telling management “students approve”, we need a test that answers: is the difference real, or chance?

Ask: is an average of 3.7 from a small sample enough to move whole courses online? Take a few answers, then say: a hypothesis test is the tool that settles it.
