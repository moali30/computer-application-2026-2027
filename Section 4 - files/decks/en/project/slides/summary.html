Wrap-up

What to remember

1

Hypotheses first

H0: no difference. H1: a difference. Write both before running SPSS.

2

Which test?

A mean against a fixed value: One Sample. The same people before and after: Paired Samples.

3

The decision rule

α = 1 − confidence. Sig < α: reject H0. Sig ≥ α: accept H0.
