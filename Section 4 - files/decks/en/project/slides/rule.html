The procedure

Three steps and one decision rule

1

State the hypotheses (H0 and H1)

2

Run the test in SPSS

3

Compare Sig with the significance level α

Significance level α = 1 − confidence level. 95% confidence means α = 0.05; 99% means α = 0.01.

Sig ≥ α

Accept H0, reject H1

The difference in the sample could be chance.

Sig < α

Reject H0, accept H1

The difference is real (significant), not chance.
